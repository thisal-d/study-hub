# ⚡ Lecture 08: Introduction to Project Management — Short Note

> **🎯 In 15 Seconds:** A project is a **temporary endeavor** undertaken to create a **unique product, service, or result**. Unlike ongoing operational routines, projects have a definite beginning and end, defined scope, and dedicated resources.

---

### 📊 1. Projects vs. Operations

| Feature | Project (ව්‍යාපෘතිය) | Operations (දෛනික මෙහෙයුම්) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | Temporary (definite start and finish dates). | Ongoing, permanent, repetitive. |
| **Output** | Unique deliverable (e.g., new mobile banking app). | Standardized output (e.g., daily cash transactions). |
| **Team Structure** | Cross-functional, often disbands at completion. | Stable functional departments (IT Support, HR, Finance). |
| **Uncertainty** | Higher risk; requirements often evolve. | Predictable; governed by Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs). |
| **Success Metric** | Value created, budget, schedule, requirements met. | High efficiency, process consistency, SLA uptime. |

---

### 📊 2. The 5 PMBOK Process Groups & 8 Performance Domains

```
   PMBOK 5 PROCESS GROUPS:
   [1. Initiating] ──► [2. Planning] ──► [3. Executing] ──► [5. Closing]
                              ▲                 │
                              │                 ▼
                              └── [4. Monitoring & Controlling]
```

- **5 Process Groups:**
  1. **Initiating:** Authorizing the project (Project Charter, identify stakeholders).
  2. **Planning:** Establishing scope, schedule, budget, quality, and risk plans.
  3. **Executing:** Coordinating people and resources to build deliverables.
  4. **Monitoring & Controlling:** Tracking performance against baselines and managing change.
  5. **Closing:** Formally completing all activities, archiving records, and releasing resources.
- **8 PMBOK Performance Domains:**
  1. Stakeholders | 2. Team | 3. Development Approach & Lifecycle | 4. Planning | 5. Project Work | 6. Delivery | 7. Measurement | 8. Uncertainty.

---

### 📊 3. Predictive (Waterfall) vs. Adaptive (Agile) at a Glance

| Feature | Predictive / Waterfall | Adaptive / Agile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Requirements** | Fixed and defined upfront. | Evolve through sprint backlogs. |
| **Delivery Model** | Single final release at project end. | Frequent working increments (every 1–4 weeks). |
| **Change Control** | Formal Change Control Board (CCB). | Embraced; Product Owner reprioritizes backlog. |
| **Customer Role** | Involved mainly at milestones & UAT. | Continuous collaboration at every Sprint Review. |

### 📚 4. Jargon Buster

| Difficult Term | Plain English Meaning | Sinhala Term |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Project Charter** | A document that formally authorizes a project and gives the PM authority. | ව්‍යාපෘති ප්‍රඥප්තිය |
| **Triple Constraint** | The traditional balancing act between Scope, Time, and Cost (Iron Triangle). | ත්‍රිත්ව බාධකය |
| **Baseline** | An approved plan (scope, schedule, cost) used as a benchmark for comparison. | පාදක මට්ටම |
| **Deliverable** | Any unique, verifiable product, result, or capability produced to complete a phase. | ප්‍රතිඵලය / භාරදිය යුතු දෙය |

### ⚠️ Common Exam Traps

> [!CAUTION]
> 1. **Process Groups are NOT Project Phases:** Process Groups (Initiating, Planning, Executing, Monitoring/Controlling, Closing) happen *within* every single phase of a project lifecycle.
> 2. **Project Success goes beyond the Iron Triangle:** Delivering on time and within budget is **not** successful if the software is bug-ridden or end users refuse to adopt it.

### 🎯 Active Recall Self-Test

<details>
<summary><b>1. What are the two defining characteristics of any project?</b></summary>

A project is **temporary** (has a distinct start and finish date) and creates a **unique** product, service, or result.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>2. Why is software maintenance usually classified as operations rather than a project?</b></summary>

Because routine maintenance (bug fixing, patch updates, user helpdesk) is ongoing, repetitive, and does not have a predetermined end date.
</details>
